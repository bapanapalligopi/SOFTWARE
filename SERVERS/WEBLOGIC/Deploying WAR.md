## **Deploy it on WebLogic**

1. Open `http://localhost:7001/console`.
2. Login as administrator.
3. Go to **Deployments → Install**.
4. Select the downloaded `helloworld.war`.
5. Choose **Install this deployment as an application**.
6. Select `ManagedServer1`.
7. Click **Finish**.
8. Select the application and click **Start → Servicing all requests**.

## **Test your application**
